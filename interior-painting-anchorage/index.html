Plan the rooms and finish you want

Interior Painting Anchorage can mean updating one bedroom, several connected spaces, or the inside of a commercial property. Before you request an estimate, make a list of the rooms and surfaces you want addressed. Include walls, ceilings, doors, trim, cabinets, or drywall repairs if they’re part of your plans. A specific list helps you talk through the scope instead of relying on a broad description like repaint the main floor.

Think about how each room is used and what you want the finished space to feel like. You don’t need every color or detail settled before you call, but examples and notes can help explain your preferences. If you’re unsure whether a damaged area needs repair before painting, point it out during the estimate.

What to expect before painting begins

A good plan accounts for the condition of the surfaces, any repair needs, and the areas you want painted. Campbell Painting LLC can discuss drywall repair as part of an interior project and cabinet refinishing when cabinets are on your list. The estimate is a useful time to review what you’ve noticed and confirm which items are included.

Before the crew arrives, ask how to prepare each room. Depending on the agreed scope, you may need to move smaller belongings, clear access to walls or cabinets, and set aside anything that shouldn’t be disturbed. Don’t assume every surface or adjacent room is included. Confirm the work area and any exclusions in advance.

A few practical points to review:

  • List each room and surface you want considered.
  • Point out drywall damage or cabinet areas that need attention.
  • Ask what you should move or protect before work starts.
  • Confirm the agreed scope and how access will be arranged.

Avoid choosing a contractor based only on a brief phone description if you haven’t discussed repairs, trim, or cabinets. Those details can change what a project involves. Call a professional when you’re not sure whether a surface is ready for paint or needs repair first.
