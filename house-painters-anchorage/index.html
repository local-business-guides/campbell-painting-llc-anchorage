What the house painting project can include

The work depends on the surfaces and rooms you want addressed. You can discuss a single room, several interior spaces, exterior painting, drywall repairs, or cabinet refinishing. If several kinds of work are involved, explain how you’d like the finished project to look so the scope can be discussed together.

A useful preparation checklist:

  • List the rooms and exterior areas you want included.
  • Make a note of drywall damage, peeling areas, or cabinets you’re considering refinishing.
  • Choose a few paint colors or finishes to discuss, if you have them in mind.
  • Ask what you should move or make accessible before work begins.
  • Confirm which surfaces are included in the estimate.

Don’t assume a repair is included just because it’s near a painted area. Bring it up while the work is being planned. A professional can help clarify whether it belongs in the painting project or needs a separate conversation.
