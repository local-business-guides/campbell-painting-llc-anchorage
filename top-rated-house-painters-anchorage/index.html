What to include in the project plan

The work may involve painting interior rooms, exterior surfaces, or a combination of areas. Drywall repair can address damaged spots before painting, and cabinet refinishing may be an option when the cabinets are part of the update. The exact work depends on the surfaces and your goals, so discuss each area rather than assuming every room or surface needs the same treatment.

Before a visit or estimate, prepare this checklist:
- List the rooms, surfaces, or exterior areas you want addressed.
- Point out peeling paint, dents, holes, or other visible damage.
- Note any areas that need drywall repair or cabinet refinishing.
- Identify furniture, belongings, or access needs the crew should know about.
- Think about the finish or overall look you want to achieve.

A common mistake is focusing only on color while overlooking surface condition. Another is leaving the project boundaries unclear. Ask what preparation is included and confirm which areas are part of the plan. Call a painting professional when repair needs are extensive, surfaces are difficult to reach, or you’re unsure how to prepare them.
